# Research history

The March 2026 SSRN working paper is the historical research version. Original paper-results CSVs remain at results/paper_results; the existing public SSRN record has not been replaced. A supplied original PDF is preserved in the external publication backup with its hash.

Subsequent work corrected accounting, drift-adjusted turnover, initial deployment, return/wealth distinctions and evidence provenance. The revised study added rebalanced 1/N, matched concentration constraints, cost scenarios and paired inference. Subsequent robustness analyses quantified strong equal-weight anchoring, examined inference sensitivity, reconstructed conditional crisis paths, independently checked US OLS/HAC calculations and added a common-calendar comparison. Unfavorable and nonsignificant findings remain reported.

Current empirical results are consolidated in [Results](RESULTS.md); the underlying tables and figures are retained in `results/phase3_final/`. Supplementary diagnostics and a manuscript candidate are retained in `outputs/final_professor_challenge_resolution/`. Prior manuscripts, manifests and specialist reviews remain at their existing paths because they are linked by checksums, traceability and historical documentation. They are not competing current releases. Earlier statements about missing daily crisis paths are superseded by the conditional reconstruction; missing target-selection histories remain unresolved.

The original working-paper version and later research revisions remain distinguishable through the repository's version history and retained evidence. The original numerical results are not presented as reproduced by the revised implementation.

Material AI involvement remains disclosed. Author declarations, independent human review and SSRN/AI-policy clearance remain pending. The repository version is distinct from an accepted or formally published revised manuscript.
