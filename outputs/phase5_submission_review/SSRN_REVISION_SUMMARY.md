# Proposed SSRN revision summary

For author approval; proposed revision of existing paper 6479462. This text describes changes from the March 2026 working paper, not a completed upload.

This revision replaces the initial empirical presentation with a separately verified and traceable specification for the United States, China and India ETF baskets. It corrects the effective samples and USD-listed instrument descriptions and explicitly identifies the Black–Litterman prior as an equal-weight equilibrium proxy with a mild historical-return tilt.

The revised comparison adds periodically rebalanced 1/N and matched 40% maximum target weights. Portfolio accounting now clearly distinguishes drifting holdings, initial deployment, target-weight instability, traded L1 turnover, modeled proportional fees, arithmetic annualized returns and compounded terminal wealth. Cost sensitivity covers the predefined 0, 5, 10 and 20-bps scenarios without changing the underlying investment decisions.

The results show substantially lower BL target instability and modeled turnover than unrestricted historical-mean Markowitz, but investment outcomes nearly match periodic equal weighting. Concentration constraints reduce Markowitz instability and turnover, while risk-adjusted rankings vary across markets. No statistically significant paired Sharpe differences were established under the specified approximate bootstrap; these findings do not establish strategy equivalence.

Crisis tests are identified as separate frozen-holdings stress evaluations with a precisely defined start-NAV recovery target. Their outcomes are event-specific and do not establish universal downside protection. Factor analysis is restricted to its verified US sample, and fixed-tau diagnostics are distinguished from the main training-only adaptive rule. Unsupported ownership-based findings are withdrawn because their original dated inputs cannot be verified.

The revision improves source/version provenance, result traceability, limitations and disclosure of AI assistance. It does not claim exact reproduction of the March numerical results. The October manuscript remains subject to author review and approval of its methods, interpretations, bibliography and declarations before any SSRN revision action.
