## Scope and actual edits

All prose was read in manuscript order, including captions, source notes, references and declarations. The revision preserves the model, evidence, unfavorable results, uncertainty qualifications and material AI disclosure. The recorded comparison with Phase 6 contains 71 changed body paragraphs; that count includes title/date formatting and terminology punctuation, not 71 substantive rewrites. Unchanged passages were reviewed and retained where already precise. Sentence lengths were varied for clarity, without mechanically enforcing a 12–20-word range or trying to obtain an AI-detector classification.

## Examples of actual wording changes

| Before | After | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| “The primary research objective of this study is to evaluate portfolio-weight instability … and assess the effectiveness … across distinct investment baskets.” | “This study evaluates portfolio-weight instability in mean–variance optimization and the effects of Black–Litterman equilibrium anchoring across investment baskets.” | Direct purpose, less nominal phrasing; no assumption of effectiveness |
| “The rule was not selected in Phase 4 to improve performance.” | “The rule was not tuned during manuscript preparation to improve performance.” | Removes an internal phase label while retaining the no-retuning statement |
| “Full coefficients and HAC inference are reported rather than inferring their signs from a figure alone.” | “Table 7 reports the full coefficients and HAC inference.” | Direct, specific evidence reference |
| “These distinctions are necessary to keep a cross-market descriptive study from claiming more identification than its design supplies.” | “The descriptive design limits the mechanisms that can be inferred from these comparisons.” | Shorter statement of the same identification limit |
| “These are software and internal-consistency evidence, not proof that a selected empirical model is economically optimal.” | “These checks support software correctness and internal consistency. They do not establish the economic optimality of the selected model.” | Clearer grammar and separate qualification |
| “BL performs nearly like periodically rebalanced equal weighting, and a reasonable matched concentration cap materially reduces the comparator's instability.” | “BL performs similarly to periodically rebalanced equal weighting. The matched concentration cap materially reduces Markowitz instability.” | Names the comparator and removes the evaluative adjective “reasonable” |
